# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
Critique ideas and code, not people. Help newcomers understand the evidence and
constraints behind a decision.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption,
and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. Do not share
sensitive captures or credentials, including to demonstrate a technical point.

The repository owner moderates project spaces and may edit/remove content, close
threads, or restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be based on
observed behavior and proportionate to its impact. The same expectations apply
to maintainers and contributors.

For a sensitive conduct concern, request a private contact without naming the
affected person in public. Do not post personal details or retaliate against
someone who raises a concern in good faith.
